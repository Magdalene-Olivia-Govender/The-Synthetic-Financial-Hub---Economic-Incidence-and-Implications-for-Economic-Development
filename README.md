# The-Synthetic-Financial-Hub---Economic-Incidence-and-Implications-for-Economic-Development
The Synthetic Financial Centre and South Africa's Financial Development: Assessing Domestic Value Creation, Intermediation and Regulatory Safeguards

Research question and Objectives
To what extent has South Africa's historical growth in cross-border financial-services activity been associated with domestic financial-sector output, and what policy conditions could help the proposed Synthetic Financial Centre translate international financial activity into broader domestic economic benefits?
Research Objectives
Baseline Relationship Dynamic: The assessment of the relationship between cross-border financial-services activity and domestic financial-sector output.
Policy condition: Would the proposed SFC generate domestic employment benefits, rather than merely increasing financial activity or output?
Does an increase in domestic financial output translate into increased long-term professional employment?
What is the association between increased financial sector activity and the growth in inequality? [Correlation + Regression Analysis]
